# Funi Raccoon Game

**The Crayon Eating Company · 2026 · PlayStation 5 · ~13 hours**

Reference sheet: `Attachments/Documents/Reference Sheets/Ficha Técnica Funi Raccoon Game.pdf`

## The loose end, resolved

This game had a reference sheet listed in GDD §9.1 but appeared in no reference list, and nobody could say whether it was a real reference or a leftover file. **It is real** — the sheet exists and the team wrote it. It should be added to §1.6.

## What the sheet records

- **Storyline:** explore a series of whimsical interconnected worlds and steal everything not nailed down. Make a garbage bin a garbage home
- **Genre:** 3D platformer, adventure. Single player
- **Audience:** casual players who like raccoons
- **Key features:** "the 2000's vibes"
- **Mechanics:** 3D movement, jump, throwing 3D items
- **Dynamics:** collecting pieces of rubbish
- **Aesthetic:** chaotic visuals referencing the 2000s

## Why it is relevant

Two things. The **2000s aesthetic**, which is the same well Frutiger Aero comes from. And the **collection loop** — the more stuff you have, the more places you can go. That is structurally identical to ARCH's gate: collect files, unlock the final level.

The difference is the polarity. Their collection is acquisitive, filling a home with things. ARCH's is subtractive — deleting to free space. Same mechanic, opposite meaning, and ARCH's version carries the theme.
