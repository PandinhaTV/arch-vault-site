# Astro Bot

**Sony / Team Asobi · 2024 · PlayStation 5 · ~13 hours**
Game designer: Gento Morita. Director and producer: Nicolas Doucet.

Reference sheet: `Attachments/Documents/Reference Sheets/Ficha Técnica - AB.pdf`

Not listed in GDD §1.6 despite the team having written a sheet for it.

## Why it is a useful reference

Three things in the sheet map directly onto ARCH's structure:

**A main hub.** Same shape as ARCH's world — a central space the player returns to, with levels reached from it.

**Unlockable skins.** 327 collectible bots the player can swap between. ARCH does the same thing with skins, and has the same problem to solve: making a cosmetic collection worth hunting. See [[Skins]].

**Gimmicks that change the moveset.** Each level introduces a mechanic that alters how the player moves. ARCH deliberately does the opposite — one moveset, available from the start, with levels changing the demands rather than the abilities. Worth knowing that the alternative was on the table.

## What the sheet records

- **Storyline:** an Astro bot crosses the galaxy recovering the parts of its console-ship after an alien attack
- **Key features:** main hub, 327 swappable bots, levels with unique gimmicks, 80 levels across 6 galaxies
- **Mechanics:** 3D movement, glide jumps, attacks, moveset-changing gimmicks, controllable camera, unlockable skins
- **Dynamics:** collecting bots, air attacks to extend hang time, gliding for shortcuts
- **Aesthetic:** colourful, fluid animation, a hub full of references to other games

## What not to take

The hub full of references to other franchises works because Sony owns them — which is the whole reason that approach does not transfer to a team that owns nothing. %% The skins decision this points at is in the Decisions folder — deliberately not linked, because this note is published. %%
