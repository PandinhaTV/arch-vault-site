# Eternity Egg

**USERLANDS · 2025 · Windows · solo developer · ~4 hours**

Reference sheet: `Attachments/Documents/Reference Sheets/Ficha Técnica - EternityEgg-1.pdf`

## Why this one matters most

Of every reference the team collected, this is the closest to what ARCH actually is — and **it is not listed in GDD §1.6**.

Its moveset, from the sheet: bounces, wall running, dashes, slides, ground pounds. Fast and fluid. That is ARCH's traversal kit almost item for item. Its aesthetic is described as "renders CG dos anos 90/2000" — 90s and 2000s CG renders, which is the neighbouring room to Frutiger Aero.

A 3D platformer with the same moveset and roughly the same visual era, made by one person in 2025. That makes it the most useful comparison the team has: it shows what this exact combination feels like when finished.

## What the sheet records

- **Storyline:** a chaotic platform-adventure with bouncy movement, grotesque characters and high-energy music
- **Genre:** 3D platformer, adventure. Single player
- **Audience:** exploration fans, teenagers
- **Key features:** distinctive character movement, distinctive visuals
- **Mechanics:** 3D movement, pogo-stick jumps, attacks, a lock-on feature, controllable camera
- **Dynamics:** using air attacks to travel further
- **Premise:** the protagonist is captured by a villain and transported to another dimension, and has to escape

## Worth investigating

- [ ] Play it. It is four hours and it is the nearest thing to ARCH that exists finished.
- [ ] How does it handle the wall-run-to-dash-to-slide chain? ARCH's [[Movement Feel Philosophy]] solved this one way; a solo dev solved it another.
- [ ] "Uso de ataques no ar para ir mais longe" — attacking in the air to travel further. ARCH's attack is radial and momentum-preserving; does theirs grant distance? See [[Dash & Attack]].
- [ ] Add it to §1.6. It belongs there more than some of the games already listed.
