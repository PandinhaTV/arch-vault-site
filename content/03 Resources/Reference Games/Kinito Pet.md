## Likely relevance

It is the odd one out among the references, and probably the most useful one for [[ARCH (the system)]]'s tone.

It is a horror game shaped like a late-90s desktop assistant — friendly software that turns out to be watching you. That is ARCH's premise played straight instead of as satire.

Worth studying for how it makes a cheerful interface feel wrong, since ARCH needs that unease without losing its brightness. Compare the four-word atmosphere brief in [[ARCH (the system)]].
