# A Hat in Time

**Gears for Breakfast · 2017 · PC, PS4, Xbox One, Switch · ~9–10 hours · PEGI 7**
Game designer: Jonas Kaerlev. Publisher: Humble Bundle. Engine: Unreal Engine 3.

Reference sheet: `Attachments/Documents/Reference Sheets/Ficha Técnica A Hat In Time.pdf`
Also the source of the Hat Kid skin — see [[Skins]].

## What the sheet records

- **Storyline:** Hat Kid, an alien trying to get home, has her magical Time Pieces scattered across a planet after a run-in with the Mafia. She explores several worlds to recover them
- **Key features:** collecting different hats, parkour, boss fights, unlockable stickers
- **Mechanics:** movement (jump, dash, run), using powers, hitting with an umbrella
- **Dynamics:** using the right movement and power at the right moment
- **Aesthetic:** the tone of each world matches the life and feel of that world

## What ARCH takes from it

**Tone.** It is funny and warm without feeling weightless. That is close to what ARCH wants — satire that does not curdle into cynicism.

**Per-world tonal consistency.** The sheet's note that "the tone of each world matches the vibe and life in it" is exactly what ARCH does with its three sub-aesthetics. See [[Art Direction - Frutiger Aero]].

## The difference worth noting

Hat Kid's hats **grant abilities**. ARCH's skins are **purely visual** — confirmed 2026-07-31, nothing about a skin changes the hitbox. So the collection serves a different purpose here: in A Hat in Time collecting expands what you can do, in ARCH it only expands who you look like.

That puts more weight on ARCH's skins being desirable in themselves, which is worth remembering while designing them. %% Full reasoning is in the Decisions folder — deliberately not linked here, because this note is published. %%
