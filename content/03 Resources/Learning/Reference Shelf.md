# Reference Shelf

The seven books in the vault, what each is actually for, and how good the copy is.

PDFs are in `Attachments/Documents/Books/` and are **excluded from git** — they are copyrighted texts and the vault is shared. Notes about them are in `03 Resources/Learning/Books` and are committed. → [[00 README - Books]]

**I use these as reference when a question suits them, and cite book and chapter when I do.** When something is my own reasoning rather than a source, I say so.

## Design

| Book | Author | For | Copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[The Art of Game Design - A Book of Lenses]] | Schell, 2008 | 100 lenses — questions to hold against the game. Pick the lens for what you are stuck on | Full, 518pp. Questions verified against the PDF by `check-lenses.py` |

## Narrative

| Book | Author | For | Copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[The Game Narrative Toolbox]] | Heussner et al., 2023 | **The process.** Concept → world → characters → story → implementation. Exercises every chapter | Full, 328pp |
| [[Game Writing - Narrative Skills for Videogames]] | Bateman (ed.), 2021 | **The theory.** Narrative vs story, the spine, freedom vs clarity, traits vs characteristics | Full, 457pp |
| [[The Advanced Game Narrative Toolbox]] | Heussner (ed.), 2019 | Production side — scheduling narrative work, research and consultants, adaptation | Full, 233pp. Skimmed only |

Worked into a method at [[How to write a game story]], with [[How to write a story]] for the craft underneath it.

## Audio

| Book | Author | For | Copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[The Game Music Toolbox]] | Aristopoulos, 2023 | **20 case studies** with harmonic analysis. Silence as technique, monothematic scoring, music as information | Full, 213pp |
| [[Writing Interactive Music for Video Games]] | Sweet, 2014 | The vocabulary of adaptive music — horizontal resequencing, vertical remixing, transitions | ⚠ **Scan, no text layer.** Obsidian search finds nothing inside it |
| [[Composing Music for Games]] | Chance Thomas, 2016 | — | ⚠ **38-page review excerpt, not the book.** No technique chapters. Nothing usable |

## What to reach for

| Stuck on | Book | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Is this fun / is this working | Schell | Whichever lens fits |
| What order do I write a story in | Game Narrative Toolbox | ch. 2–5 |
| The player keeps ignoring my story | Bateman | ch. 4, 5 |
| A character feels flat | Bateman ch. 6, then Toolbox ch. 4 | traits before characteristics |
| Music sounds too full | Game Music Toolbox | ch. 11 — subtract |
| Music should react to play | Sweet | ch. 8, 9 |
| A level needs a musical identity | Game Music Toolbox | ch. 10, 13 |
| Estimating narrative or audio work | Advanced Toolbox | ch. 11 |

## Gaps on this shelf

Nothing here covers level design, 3D platformer movement, or production for a student team. Those have come up more than any audio question has.
