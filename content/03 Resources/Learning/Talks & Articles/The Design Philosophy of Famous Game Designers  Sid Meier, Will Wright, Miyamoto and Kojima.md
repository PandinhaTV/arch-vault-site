> **Each designer in the video is one lens**, which is why it reads like Schell:
>
> | Designer | Their lens |
> |---|---|
> | Sid Meier | Games are a series of interesting decisions |
> | Will Wright | Games are systems — layers, feedback loops |
> | Jonathan Blow | Meaning emerges from mechanics, not script |
> | Clint Hocking | The world's logic governs everything, even the un-fun parts |
> | Warren Spector | Show the goal; give problems, not puzzles |
> | Fumito Ueda | Design by subtraction — strip whatever doesn't serve the mood |
> | Miyamoto | Form follows function; the avatar must feel good before the world exists |
>
