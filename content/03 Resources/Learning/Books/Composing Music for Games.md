- **What the contents promise**, for deciding whether to get hold of it properly: a *Perspective* section on the vocabulary of scoring, three chapters of principles including one specifically on what distinguishes *video game* scoring from film, two technique chapters, three process chapters (composition, production, implementation), and then four chapters of business
