# Nexus

The corporation that built [[ARCH (the system)]]. It is the real antagonist of the story, and it never appears as a character.

## Public image

Innovation and sustainability. Modern, green, and apparently concerned with employee wellbeing.

## What it actually does

Excessive hours, constant monitoring, and an abusive work culture.

- [[Bloatwares]] monitor employee efficiency
- [[Peeps]] maintain the illusion of support
- [[Johnny Boss]] is sent when a defect is found in an employee's behaviour

## Why it has no face

The player's rebellion is deleting files, which is a small administrative act. Giving Nexus a physical character would set up a confrontation the game does not offer, and would make the ending about beating a person instead of getting time back.

Instead the company is present in the environment: the tower on the horizon, the office assets, and the Bright Tertiaires palette appearing in the world. This is how the aesthetic conflict does narrative work. See [[Art Direction - Frutiger Aero]].
