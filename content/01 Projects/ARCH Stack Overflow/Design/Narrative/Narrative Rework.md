# Narrative Rework

The team decided on 2026-08-03 to redo the narrative to be more engaging, possibly adding more NPCs. → [[Meeting 2026-08-03]]

This note is the working space for that. It is not a rewrite yet.

## What exists now

The story is sound and thin. [[Premise & Synopsis]] has a clear premise, seven plot points, and a backstory — and almost no scenes. One cutscene exists, the opening. There is no middle.

The seven plot points from §3.1.2 include *"He gradually discovers the true nature of Nexus"*, and nothing in the game currently delivers that. It is the beat the rework most obviously needs.

## The idea already on the table

From the meeting: **NPCs added to the hub as the player progresses**, so the world fills up over time.

That is worth taking seriously for three reasons. It gives progress a visible shape without a progress bar. It gives the [[Peeps]] a reason to become more interactive, which the meeting also asked for. And it fits the premise — freeing disk space is what makes room for things, so a hub that fills as you delete is the mechanic and the metaphor doing the same work.

The question it raises: where do the NPCs come from? Files you *didn't* delete? Workers who escaped like you did? Programs that were taking up space and now have room to exist?

## What to protect

The rework should not lose these. They are the parts that already work.

- **Deleting is the rebellion.** The joke and the theme are the same act. Any new scene that does not touch taking space back is decoration.
- **[[Nexus]] never appears as a character.** An antagonist you cannot punch is the right shape for a story about reclaiming an afternoon.
- **Nobody speaks in words.** Grunts throughout, settled 2026-07-31. It costs nothing to localise and it keeps the tone light. A narrative rework that adds dialogue undoes that quietly.
- **The tone is calm and slightly wrong**, not sinister. See [[ARCH (the system)]].
