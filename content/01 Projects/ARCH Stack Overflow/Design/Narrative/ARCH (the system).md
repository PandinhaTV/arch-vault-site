# ARCH (the system)

The operating system built by [[Nexus]]. The game takes place inside it.

## Atmosphere

Futuristic technology combined with 2000s Frutiger Aero, visually close to Solarpunk. §3.2.2 says the world should feel four things at once:

- nostalgia
- tranquility
- curiosity
- strangeness

The environments are bright and welcoming, but there is constant tension from the monitoring presence and from Nexus itself.

Those four words are a useful test for anything added to the world. If a new element is none of the four, it is decoration.

## Structure

The system is organised into WorkSpaces, which are the areas Cache explores. In play these are the islands of the archipelago — see [[World Model - Islands & Landmarks]].

The world area connects the aquatic level and the final level. It is the zone with the strongest Frutiger Aero styling.

## The premise stated plainly

The system looks clean, efficient and futuristic. It is actually a manipulative environment where interfaces, rules and visual feedback shape the behaviour of the people inside it.
