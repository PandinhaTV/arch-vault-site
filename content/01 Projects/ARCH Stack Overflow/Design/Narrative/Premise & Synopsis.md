# Premise & Synopsis

## Short version

A Nexus employee wants to install a game on his work computer. The disk is full of corporate files. He enters the ARCH operating system as his avatar [[Cache]] and starts deleting them.

## Synopsis

*ARCH: Stack Overflow* follows an employee of Nexus, a large technology corporation that exploits its workers through constant monitoring and abusive hours. With no time to rest, he decides to install a game on the company computer during work hours. He finds the system completely full of old documents, reports and unnecessary corporate files.

To free space, he enters the ARCH operating system through his avatar, Cache. Inside this digital world he explores different WorkSpaces, deletes accumulated PDFs, and avoids the monitors Nexus sends after him.

## Original Portuguese (§ storyline)

> O jogador assume o papel de um utilizador dentro do sistema "ARCH", uma plataforma desenvolvida pela empresa Nexus. À primeira vista, o sistema parece limpo, eficiente e futurista, mas rapidamente se revela um ambiente manipulador onde interfaces, regras e feedback visual influenciam o comportamento do jogador. O objetivo é navegar e "deletar" a lógica do sistema para assim, liberar espaço para o player poder descansar em paz das suas tarefas corporativas.

## Plot points (§3.1.2)

1. The protagonist finds he does not have enough space to install a game.
2. He enters ARCH through the avatar Cache.
3. He explores different digital WorkSpaces.
4. He deletes accumulated corporate documents — PDFs and ZIP folders.
5. He meets Bloatwares that try to stop him.
6. He gradually discovers the true nature of Nexus.
7. He continues his rebellion against the company's control system.

## Note on the two stated goals

The synopsis says the goal is to rest from corporate tasks. §1.1 says the goal is to download a video game. These are different. One should be used everywhere. See [[Open Issues in the GDD]].
