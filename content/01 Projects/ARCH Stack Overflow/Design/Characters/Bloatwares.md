# Bloatwares

The main antagonists. Small red spheres created by Nexus to monitor how efficiently employees work.

## Behaviour

They run at the player and deal damage on contact.

In [[Aquatic]] they are placed on platforms that are larger than the standard ones. This means a wider platform tells the player a fight is coming.

## Where they are

They never share an area with the [[Peeps]] (confirmed 2026-07-31). The world separates into places with helpers and places with threats.

## Naming

![[concept-characters.png]]

The concept art labels these characters **Wanker**. So the `Wanker` class in the Unity code is not a placeholder or a slip — it is the character's actual name, and it predates the GDD's wording.

That leaves three names in circulation:

- **Bloatware** — the species/role term. §1.9.3, §3.3.1.6, most of the GDD
- **Wanker** — the character name, used in the concept art and the code
- **SpyWare** — §3.1 only, in the narrative summary

Bloatware and Wanker can coexist: one is what they are, the other is what they are called. SpyWare is the odd one out and is probably just an inconsistency.

- [x] Confirm with the team, then make §3.1 say Bloatware and keep `Wanker` in code. ✅ 2026-07-31

**Settled 2026-07-31.** Bloatware is the prose term, `Wanker` stays as the character and class name, and SpyWare is dropped. The class is not renamed — it was never wrong. Both v1.5 drafts already read this way.

[[Johnny Boss]] is a separate design. §1.9.3 calls him a kind of bloatware, but he is not a scaled-up one — he has no head, and the concept art that once labelled him "John Wanker" now reads Johnny Boss.
