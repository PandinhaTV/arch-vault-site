# Clippy

The official digital companion of the ARCH operating system.

He is found on the outside section of [[Aquatic]], just before the platforming starts. The player can interact with him. He has dialogue and a voice.

## Role

He is the character who explains things to the player. Players already expect an assistant popup to explain something, so teaching through him needs no setup.

## IP note

This is Microsoft's Clippy, essentially unchanged. It fits the 2000s operating system setting, but it is someone else's character.

This is the same question as [[Skins]]. It is usually accepted in academic work and becomes a problem if the game is published. A renamed and redesigned paperclip would keep the joke. The team should decide rather than leave it as it is. See [[Open Issues in the GDD]].
