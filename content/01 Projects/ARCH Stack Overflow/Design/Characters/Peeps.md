# Peeps

Small helpers for Nexus employees. They were created to support the illusion that the company cares about its workers.

They were built as public relations, and they help Cache anyway. The GDD does not develop this idea further.

## In game

They give guidance and support during exploration.

They have 2 waving animations, 5 idles and a swim, so they are mostly ambient presence rather than a system.

## Gold Peeps

The NPCs in difficult situations that §2.1.2 says point the player toward secret items **are Peeps, painted gold**.

That makes gold a signal the player learns once and reads forever: a gold Peep means a secret is nearby. It costs one recolour of an existing asset, which is about as cheap as a collectible signpost gets.

## Where they are

Peeps and [[Bloatwares]] never share an area. That is a zoning rule, not a coincidence — the world separates into places with helpers and places with threats.

## Direction change 2026-08-03

The team decided the Peeps should be **more interactive and react more to the player**, as part of making the narrative more engaging. There is also an idea to add NPCs to the hub over time, so it fills up as the player progresses. → [[Meeting 2026-08-03]]

That sits oddly with the answer from 31 July, that they do not have many thoughts and do not react to Bloatwares. The two can be reconciled — a creature that reacts without understanding is a better joke than one that does neither — but the older answer should be read as out of date.

## Answered

*Questions that got settled. Keep the answer, not just the tick.*

- **Do they know they are helping Cache break the rules?** *(2026-07-31)* — No. They do not have many thoughts; they are somewhat dumb.
- **Do they react to Bloatwares?** *(2026-07-31)* — No, and they are never in the same area as them.
- **Are the secret-item NPCs from §2.1.2 Peeps?** *(2026-07-31)* — Yes. They are Peeps painted gold.
- **Are gold Peeps in the open archipelago, on the islands, or both?** *(2026-07-31)* — Both. They sit in the middle of the islands and out in the [[Archipelago]].
- **Does a gold Peep stay gold after its secret is found?** *(2026-07-31)* — Yes. It stays gold; it only ever indicates that a secret is nearby.
- **Is the safe/hostile zoning consistent enough that players learn to read it?** *(2026-07-31)* — Not yet settled. It is a constraint to keep in mind when designing the levels rather than something the design already guarantees.
