# Cache

The player character. He is the digital avatar of a Nexus employee inside the ARCH system.

## Backstory

Cache represents Nexus employees within ARCH. In the story he is controlled by an angry worker who wants some leisure time back from a company that overworks him. The worker finds the company computer full of unnecessary files, so he enters the system to delete them and install the game he wants.

## Personality

He seems relaxed and unbothered, but he is determined. He does not look for attention. He goes through the obstacles Nexus puts in front of him to get his freedom.

## Appearance

![[concept-characters.png]]

Frutiger Aero mixed with retro-futurism. Simple shapes, expressive silhouette, energetic style.

**His head is a mouse cursor.** The concept art shows it clearly — the white arrow pointer sits where a head would be. This is the single most distinctive thing in his design and the GDD never states it in words.

The main visual reference is Canti from the anime FLCL — see [[FLCL]]. The influence shows in the simple shapes and the strong visual identity.

The cursor head should survive any restyle. It is tied to the operating-system premise rather than to a visual era, and it is the single most distinctive thing in the design.

## Abilities

Run, double jump, wall jump, wall run, dash, grappling hook, ground pound, slide, attack, and float using his head.

His head does three jobs. It is his head; it becomes the hoverboard, dropping to his feet when he rides and returning when he stops; and it turns into a pointing hand when something can be interacted with.

The head-float listed in §1.3 is not a separate ability — it is the hoverboard. Settled 2026-07-30. See [[Hoverboard]].

## Stats

High mobility and reach. Weak resilience — the player is expected to avoid damage through skill rather than absorb it.

## Animation

Fast, fluid and expressive. Simple but dynamic poses, to convey energy and lightness.

Cache has 30 animations. The full list is in [[GDD v1.4 - Source Text (PT)]] §10.1.1.2.
