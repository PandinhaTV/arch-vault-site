# Planície

The playground island. No objective, no folder, no gate — somewhere to try the moveset with nothing at stake.

Replaced the old `Tutorial` note on 2026-08-12, which described this as an opening tutorial. It is not one. → [[Game Structure]]

## Why this is Planície

`source: inferred` — **not confirmed by the team.**

The opening cutscene names four islands: Aquática, Vertical, Final, Planície. The team's plan names four areas: aquatic, vertical, final, and a playground with no objective. Three match by name. Planície is what is left, and *planície* means flatland — which is what a practice area looks like.

Before this, Planície was the vault's oldest unanswered question: an island promised by a cutscene with nothing behind it, and the obvious thing to cut. If this reading is right it is not a loose end at all, and cutting it would remove the only place in the WorkSpace where a player can fail for free.

- [ ] Confirm with the team. One sentence in the meeting settles it.

## It is not a tutorial

This WorkSpace sits mid-game, after every mechanic is unlocked. A playground that comes after you already own everything is a sandbox, not a tutorial. The difference matters:

- A **tutorial** teaches a move to someone who has never held the controller. It needs a teaching order, safety, and a reason to exist in the fiction.
- A **sandbox** lets someone practise a move they already own. It needs space, forgiving geometry, and nothing to lose.

This is the second one. Whatever teaching the full game needs happens in an earlier WorkSpace that does not exist yet.

## The gym scene is a different thing

In the Unity project there is a gym or test sandbox scene. That is a developer tool — somewhere to check that dash still feels right after a change. It is not this.

One scene was doing both jobs. Keeping the gym developer-only and building Planície as a player-facing island separates them.

## What it needs

Nothing is designed yet. What the role implies:

- Flat ground, generous surfaces, no death planes near the middle
- One of everything the moveset touches — a wall to run, a hook point, a ramp, a ring, a ground-pound button
- A view of at least one other island, so it works as an opening viewpoint
- No collectible on the critical path
