# Aquatic

**Objective:** collect the folder at the top of the aquatic tower.
**Theme:** flow.
**Sub-aesthetic:** Helvetica Aqua Aero.
**Walkthrough:** exterior → tower interior → tower top.

## Physical description

![[concept-aquatic-world.png]]

Vibrant tropical colours over a dominant blue. The palette appears in the layout, which is water, and in the assets — shells, corals, transparent coloured platforms, seaweed, rings and fish.

It also carries elements of the parent aesthetic: bubbles, green tones, and assets outside the island that can be seen from inside it. Those include the Frutiger Aero wind turbines and the Nexus building in Bright Tertiaires.

That last detail means the Final island is visible from inside this one, which is the landmark model working. → [[World Model - Islands & Landmarks]]

## Mechanical focus

Building speed. Grappling hook, bubble rings and slippery ramps.

## Structure

The sections are divided using kishōtenketsu — introduction, development, twist, conclusion. See [[Kishotenketsu Level Structure]].

The twist section is not identified anywhere in the GDD.

## The two paths

The island has two routes.

The normal route has the player press all the buttons and progress naturally.

The second route is for a player who already knows the layout. Every button and its associated wall can be avoided completely.

The buttons deliberately break flow, because pressing one forces Cache to stop and ground pound. That friction is intentional, and knowing the island lets a player skip all of it. Recorded in [[Decision - Buttons can be skipped to preserve flow]].

## Encounters

[[Clippy]] is on the exterior, before the platforming starts. He has dialogue and a voice.

[[Bloatwares]] are spread through the island, on platforms larger than the standard ones.
