# Archipelago

⚠ **This note is not verified.** It carries over what the old "World Hub" note said, which was mostly §1.9.1's description of *o Mundo*. On 2026-08-12 I also wrote that it was open water the player crossed between islands. **That was invented and is wrong** — no such water exists. → [[Corrections Log]]

What is left is what §1.9.1 actually claims: a part of the WorkSpace called *o Mundo*, holding basic enemies and exploration zones, carrying the strongest Frutiger Aero styling. Whether that is a place, the outdoor surface of the islands, or a term the GDD uses loosely is **not established**.

Renamed from "World Hub" on 2026-08-12. It is not the hub. The hub is a separate space where the player picks a WorkSpace, and it has no note yet because it has no design yet. → [[Game Structure]]

## Purpose

It holds basic enemies and exploration zones, and carries the strongest Frutiger Aero styling — it defines what the game looks like in a screenshot.

**It does not connect the islands.** Portals do that. → [[Game Structure]] · [[Vertical Slice Flow.excalidraw|Vertical Slice Flow]]

## What it does for the player

It is where players chain abilities freely, because nothing here gates the moveset.

It holds PDFs and ZIPs to collect.

It is what players in [[Playtest 01]] wanted more of — the finding the whole project turned on. → [[Decision - The world became the main attraction]]

## Contents

- Basic enemies — [[Bloatwares]]
- Exploration zones
- PDFs and ZIPs to collect
- Secret items pointed to by NPCs
- Music: *Air Runners — World*

## Its role, versus what the GDD says

§1.9.1 describes it in one line, as something that unites the levels. The playtest evidence says players treat it as the main attraction, and development was refocused onto it. The GDD has not been updated to match.

Under [[Game Structure]] it is also not "uniting levels" — the islands are areas of one level, and this is the space between them.

## Teaching

Nothing here teaches a move for the first time. This WorkSpace sits mid-game, after everything is unlocked, so the archipelago is a place to *use* the moveset rather than learn it. The practice space is [[Planície]].

That is a change from what earlier notes assumed. It also means the wall-run complaints in [[Playtest 01]] cannot be answered by teaching here.
