# Office

**Renamed from "Final Tower" on 2026-08-12**, from your flow diagram. → [[Vertical Slice Flow.excalidraw|Vertical Slice Flow]]

**Objective:** collect the folder inside the Nexus tower, which requires beating [[Johnny Boss]].
**Sub-aesthetic:** Bright Tertiaires.
**Walkthrough:** lobby → vertical interior → horizontal interior → Johnny Boss's room.

## Physical description

![[concept-final-level.png]]

Built on tertiary colours — specific tones of orange, green and blue. It also takes from Corporate aesthetics, with office assets in the Bright Tertiaires palette and white walls in places.

## Role in the WorkSpace

This is where the two halves of the game meet. Outside is Frutiger Aero: bright, organic, nostalgic. Inside the tower it becomes corporate. The player has been able to see this building since the aquatic island, and since the opening cutscene before that.

The GDD says it presents all the mechanics the map taught the player. Under [[Game Structure]] that reads slightly differently: this WorkSpace sits mid-game, so nothing is taught here for the first time. The other three islands are where the player warms the moveset up; this is where it gets tested.

## Structure

The map is annotated with Ki and Shō, the first two parts of kishōtenketsu. See [[Kishotenketsu Level Structure]].

Ten and Ketsu are not marked. Either they are unbuilt, or the boss fight is meant to be both. The team should confirm which.

## Encounters

[[Johnny Boss]] is in the final room. Entering starts the fight. Beating him gives the last folder and ends the WorkSpace.

He is the boss of this WorkSpace, not of the game, and he appears nowhere else. Settled 2026-08-12. §1.9 calls him *"o boss final do nosso jogo"* and needs correcting. → [[Game Structure]]

The implemented fight is described in [[Boss Fight Structure]].
