# Portfolio & Career

The area that gets neglected until it's urgent. Keep it fed with small deposits.

## What lives here
- Portfolio pieces and what each one demonstrates
- Devlog drafts, jam entries, talk proposals
- Contacts, communities, opportunities
- Skills you're deliberately building

> [!tip] Deposit habit
> When a project hits a milestone, write one paragraph here about what it proves you can do. Doing this at the end, from memory, is how portfolios end up vague.
